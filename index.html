About Us

PT Gratio Artha Prima — Trusted Engineering & Construction Partner

Established on May 8, 2021, in South Jakarta, we focus on delivering high-quality products, deep technical expertise, and responsive service for power generation, industrial utilities, mechanical systems, and infrastructure development. We understand that operational continuity is critical, especially in the energy sector where downtime directly impacts productivity and public services. We work with professionalism, integrity, and innovation.

PT Gratio Artha Prima provides end-to-end solutions ranging from procurement and maintenance support to engineering, construction, and project implementation. Our mission is to strengthen Indonesia's energy resilience and industrial growth by delivering dependable solutions that meet international standards and exceed customer expectations.
